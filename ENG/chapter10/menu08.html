For detailed information on tourist destinations in Korea, visit the website of the Korea Tourism Organization (www.visitkorea.or.kr) or call ☎1330. You can search the destination by theme at the homepage. The website is provided in 12 languages (Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, French, Indonesian, German, Spanish, Vietnamese, Thai, Russian, and Arabic). The 1330 Korea Travel Hotline provides information in 8 languages (Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, Vietnamese, Thai, Malay/Indonesian) 24/7 all year round (8:00 a.m.~7:00 p.m. for Vietnamese, Thai, Bahasa Melayu); text-chatting counseling services are also offered in 4 languages (Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese). You may visit the local government website for more detailed information on local tourist sites.
