01Programs for Early Adaptation of Immigrants

The 'Initial Adjustment Support Program for Immigrants is provided in 18 languages including Korean to provide information on Korea's basic legal system and social adjustment/ assimilation in helping foreigners stably settle down with daily life in Korean society at the outset of arrival. It is administered at the immigration and migrant offices and Initial Adjustment Support Center. You can apply thru the Social Integration Information Network website (www.socinet.go.kr).

Main Contents of Education

  • Local community information, response methods & contact information in case of an emergency, human rights protection, etc.

  • Requirements for permanent residence & nationality
Advantages of Completing the Immigrant Initial Adaptation Program
  • M arried immigrants are granted 2 years visa upon registration (excluding those who have entry restrictions)
  • Upon participation of the KIIP, 2 hours will be considered completed from the total hours of the whole course as your benefit.(applies to all levels)
    • Two(2) hours will be considered complete upon the designation of your first level (however, 0 level is excluded).

02Korea Immigration Integration Program (KIIP)

The Korea Immigration Integration Program facilitates the acquisition of nationality, stay qualification, and etc. for those immigrants who have completed required educational courses (Korean language and cultural courses) approved by the Ministry of Justice, in order to support married immigrants in becoming independent, successful members of Korean society.

Purpose of programs : To promote the early adaptation and successful settlement of immigrants in Korean society
Educational programs
  1. 1Korean language and culture
  2. 2Understanding of Korean society

  • Applicants who passed the comprehensive assessment for permanent residence will not take basic courses but be placed in the advanced course immediately
Operation cycle: education courses autonomously operated by institutions
Operating institution: 355 operating institutions (as of March 2024)
Benefits for Program Participants
  • When applying for naturalization
    • Recognition of Comprehensive Evaluation for Naturalization for applicants (granted when completing the naturalization program)
    • Exemption from naturalization interview screening (those who have completed the 5-step Korean immigration naturalization eligibility procedure and passed the comprehensive evaluation for naturalization)
  • When applying for permanent residence qualification
    • Recognized for fulfilling basic requirements
  • When applying for other stay qualification
    • Additional points awarded (D-8-4 Technology-Based Startup points system, D-9-1 International Trade points system, D-10-1 Job Seeker points system, E-7-4 Skilled Workers points system, F-2-7 Long-term residency point system)
    • Exempt from proving Korean language proficiency, etc.
  • When applying for a visa
    • Exempt from proving Korean language proficiency, etc.

Useful Information
‘Hi Korea’, e-government homepage for foreigners

Through the 'Hi Korea' website, which is operated in three languages, including Korean, English, & Chinese, you can apply for electronic civil petitions such as a valid visa to extend the sojourn duration and report a change in place of residence, or apply for a reservation for a visit to Korea Immigration Service (immigration offices).

  • How to Use
    Visit homepage(www.hikorea.go.kr) → Sign up for membership → — Access services such as online requests & complaints, reservations, my page, etc.
General information center for foreigners: ☎1345(no area code needed)

Provides information to residents living in Korea to help them adjust to their lifestyle by phone. It is available in 20 different languages including Korean.

  • Weekdays 09:00~22:00 (only Korean, English, and Chinese available after 18:00)
  • Website www.hikorea.go.kr
Social integration information network (SociNet) homepage

Provided are detailed information on social integration programs, international marriage info. programs, early adaptation programs for marriage immigrants, and integrated citizenship education tailored to permanent residents & naturalized citizens. You can directly apply to participate in the desired curriculum through the website.

  • How to Use
    Access the website (www.socinet.go.kr) → Register as a member → Avail services such as applying for participation in various social integration education and checking results
