01Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance

The Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance incurs a premium from the sole proprietors, and the fund is paid to employees instead of the owner. In principle, it applies to all businesses and companies that hire workers. If a worker is injured from industrial disasters,, he or she must submit an application to the Korea Workers' Compensation & welfare Service, and accident compensation wages may be provided after a review occurs.

  • Refer to Korea Workers’ Compensation & Welfare Service website – Media room – Brochure for foreigners – 2020 English Brochure

(1)Tips to Remember in the Event of an Industrial Accident

  • Employees or their families can apply for industrial accident compensation payment directly to the Korea Labor Welfare Corporation (☎1588-0075).
  • Early treatment (emergency treatment) is very critical in the event of an industrial accident. Victims must consult with an expert for diagnosis and treatment. In the case of amputation such as a severed finger, the victim must visit a hospital that provides replantation/ reattachment operation services.
  • During treatment for an industrial accident, a company cannot fire a worker or force deportation. Even if an industrial accident is attributed to a worker’s fault, the worker can still receive industrial accident compensation if an injury occurred on the job.
  • In addition, industrial accident insurance benefits will be paid only to a worker’s personal bank account, so workers should get a bank account beforehand..

(2)Qualifications

Businesses or employees of affiliated businesses governed by the Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance Act or their families can apply for compensation in the case of injury, illness, disability, or fatality while on the job.

(3)Preparation of Documents

When filling out the application form for medical care expenses, be sure to record the content and date of accident in detail and correctly. If the worker gets treated at a medical institution for an industrial accident, he or she can make a request to the medical institution to apply for industrial accident compensation payment on his or her behalf.

02Basic Safety Rules

The first step for the prevention of industrial accidents is to keep your work space and surrounding areas clean and organized. Hallways should be kept clear, and floors should be checked on a regular basis to ensure they are not slippery. The following are basic safety rules that can be applied to every workplace.

  • Work after considering the risk factors in the related work.
  • Turn off the power before cleaning, or any repair, and maintenance work.
  • Wear working clothes while working; do not smoke or eat on the work site.
  • Do not wear cotton gloves when working with a rotating machine.
  • Wear protective gear, such as safety hat and shoes.
  • Wear protective glasses when working in an area with particulates and dust during cutting, using machines, etc.
  • Harmful materials should be classified and stored in a designated location by type.
  • Wear a protective mask (gas mask) when using organic solvents like cleansers and adhesives.
  • Always be aware of the location of the nearest exit.
  • Always be aware of the location of the first-aid kit and the fire extinguisher.
  • Check all safety and hygiene messages such as prohibition·warning·instruction·information, etc.
  • Wash your hands before eating.
